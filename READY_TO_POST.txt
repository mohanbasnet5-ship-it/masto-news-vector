Nepal Rastra Bank Tightens Rules, Testing Whether Banks Can Actually Comply

Nepal Rastra Bank is pushing stricter regulations on lending, capital requirements, and risk management across the banking system, forcing lenders to choose between compliance costs and profit margins. This echoes past cycles where sudden regulatory shifts have triggered credit crunches that starved small businesses of working capital. Banks will likely raise loan interest rates or tighten eligibility, pricing out traders, farmers, and small manufacturers who depend on affordable credit. This matters because ordinary Nepalis who need loans to expand a shop or buy seed will face higher rates or outright rejection—and slower economic growth ripples back to job losses and wage stagnation in every sector.
