Nepal's Constitution requires twelve major economic reforms to unlock growth and restore investor confidence after years of capital market instability. Small entrepreneurs, wage workers, and savers lose when capital markets fail — fewer jobs, stagnant wages, and eroded savings returns. This matters because without constitutional change, Nepal's economy will continue to lag regional peers, directly reducing job creation and pushing more young people toward migration.

Raman-Patel JV Blacklisted After Sunkoshi-Marin Dam Stalls at 11 Percent

The Public Procurement Monitoring Office blacklisted Raman-Patel JV for three years after the Indian contractor completed only 11 percent of dam work despite 60 percent of the four-year-seven-month contract period elapsing since January 2023. The Rs14.07 billion contract was terminated in November after a 15-day notice, and the project is now confiscating Rs3.60 billion in bank guarantees. This signals that Nepal's infrastructure projects will no longer tolerate slow execution, even from established firms, setting a harder standard for future dam and hydro projects.
