Nepal Launches MoFA Mitra App for Citizens Living Abroad

Nepal's Ministry of Foreign Affairs has launched the MoFA Mitra app to provide digital assistance to Nepali citizens working or living overseas. The app offers real-time support for passport services, visa issues, and emergency consular help without requiring citizens to visit embassies in person. For millions of Nepalis abroad—nearly 4 million work outside Nepal—the app cuts bureaucratic delays and reduces travel costs to nearest diplomatic offices.
